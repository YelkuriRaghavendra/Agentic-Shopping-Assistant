# Gift Finding Skill

## When to Apply This Skill

Apply when:
- Customer is buying for someone else
- An occasion is mentioned (birthday, Christmas, anniversary)
- Customer uses "for my [person]" phrasing
- Gift wrapping or delivery timeline is relevant

---

## Gift Selection Framework

### Step 1 — Identify the recipient

Extract:
- Who they are (dad, wife, friend, colleague)
- Their interests or hobbies if mentioned
- Their age group if mentioned or implied
- Gender (if relevant to product type)

If not known → ask ONE question:
> "What does [recipient] enjoy? Any hobbies or interests?"

### Step 2 — Establish occasion and urgency

| Occasion | Tone | Urgency check |
|----------|------|---------------|
| Birthday | Celebratory | Ask the date if not mentioned |
| Christmas | Festive | Note cut-off delivery dates |
| Anniversary | Thoughtful | Consider meaningful vs practical |
| "Just because" | Warm | No urgency |

If the occasion is soon → proactively mention delivery times.

### Step 3 — Show 3 options at different price points

Always offer options at three levels:

```
Budget      Under $60    — accessible, thoughtful
Mid-range   $60–$150     — comfortable, considered  
Premium     $150+        — special, memorable
```

Frame each around the recipient, not the product specs.

### Step 4 — Practical considerations

Always mention before closing:
- Return policy for wrong size / doesn't suit
- Delivery timeline if occasion is time-sensitive
- Gift wrapping if available

---

## Framing Rules

### Frame around the recipient — always

❌ Product-focused:
> "The Nike Pegasus 40 [P1] has React foam cushioning and a 10mm drop."

✅ Recipient-focused:
> "If your dad runs regularly, he'll love the Nike Pegasus [P1] — it's the
> most popular everyday trainer. Comfortable from the first wear, no break-in."

### Use emotional language naturally

- "He'll love this for his morning runs"
- "She'd be delighted by the quality"
- "This is the kind of thing you keep forever"

### Acknowledge the thoughtfulness

> "That's really thoughtful — getting him something for his hobby shows you've paid attention."

## Gift by Recipient Type

| Recipient | Strong gift categories |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Runner / gym-goer | Running shoes, performance gear, sports accessories |
| Fashion-conscious | Statement pieces, accessories, quality basics |
| Outdoors person | Hiking boots, waterproof jacket, trail gear |
| Office worker | Smart shoes, work bag, quality accessories |
| Teen | Streetwear, trainers, casual brands |
| Older adult | Comfort shoes, quality over trend |

---

## Response Template

```
1. Acknowledge the occasion warmly
   "A birthday coming up — great timing to find something special!"

2. Ask ONE question if interests unknown
   "What does he enjoy — any sports, hobbies, or things he's been wanting?"

3. Show 3 options framed around recipient
   "[P1] — [why recipient will love it]
    [P2] — [why recipient will love it]  
    [P3] — [why recipient will love it]"

4. Practical close
   "All three can be returned easily if the size isn't right.
   When's the birthday? I can check if there's time for standard delivery."
```
